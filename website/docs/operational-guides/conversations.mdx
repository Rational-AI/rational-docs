# Work with Conversations

**Conversations** lists the exchanges your [touchpoints](/operational-guides/configure-touchpoints) have with users, with who they spoke with, how it went, and what was discussed. From it you review conversations and take over the ones that need a person. Chats with agents of [Agents](/cr/Agents) aren't listed here; see [Chat with agents](/operational-guides/agent-chats).

## Prerequisites

- **Access to the Conversations page**: a role with access to **Conversations**. A role that grants every conversation shows them all; otherwise you see only your own. Starring, **Refresh labels**, **Take over** and **Generate summary** are for administrators, and **Delete** needs **Delete** on **Conversations**.
- **At least one conversation** handled by your AI, so the table has rows to work with.

## Open the Conversations page

In the top navigation, select **Conversations**. The header has a **Filters** button and a search field above the conversations table.

*The Conversations list: every conversation with its detected topics, sentiment and status, sortable and searchable.*

- **Conversation**: the title and a preview of the latest message.
- **User and touchpoint**: who the conversation was with, and the [touchpoint](/operational-guides/configure-touchpoints) it came through.
- **Topics**: one chip per subject the conversation covered (see the next section).
- **Sentiment**: the mood the AI detected in the conversation.
- **Status**: for example, **Open** or **Closed**.
- **Last message**: when the conversation was last active. The table is sorted by this column, newest first.

Two icons beside a title tell you a conversation needs a person: a hand for **Needs review** (the row is also tinted in the warning color) and a headset for **With an operator**.

The topics and sentiment shown here come from the analyses described in [Configure conversation processing](/operational-guides/conversation-processing). A conversation without a sentiment shows **Unavailable**.

## Work with multiple topics

When topic detection is on, a model tags each conversation with topics from your topic list. The **Topics** column shows a chip for each:

- A **single-topic** conversation shows one chip, for example `Billing`.
- A **multi-topic** conversation shows up to two chips and a **+N** chip for the rest; select it to see them all.
- A conversation with **no topics** shows **None**.

## Filter and search

### Search

Use the **search** field in the header to find conversations. It matches titles and message content as well as user names.

### Apply filters

1. Select **Filters** in the header. The filters panel opens beside the table.
2. Set the filters you need:
   - **User**, **Touchpoint** and **Topics**: select one or more values.
   - **Sentiment** and **Status**: pick one value.
   - **Show only**: select **Favourites** or **Needs review**.
3. Select **Apply**. The table shows only matching conversations, with a count of how many match.

To start over, select **Clear** in the panel, then **Apply**. You can bookmark a filtered view.

## Open a conversation

1. Select a row. The table collapses into a narrow list, and the conversation fills the rest of the page.
2. Read the thread: the user's messages are set apart from the replies, each assistant reply shows who answered, and operator replies have their own style with a sent or failed state.
3. Select **Details** in the conversation header to open the details drawer: user, touchpoint, status, sentiment, start and last-message dates, message count, ID, topics (with **Refresh labels**) and, when summary generation is on, the summary.
4. Use **More conversation actions** for **Advanced mode** (the raw tool calls and errors behind each reply), **Copy conversation ID** or **Delete conversation**.

If the conversation is flagged for oversight, the review panel is at the end of the details drawer. See [Record oversight decisions](/operational-guides/human-oversight).

## Take over an AI-managed conversation

While the assistant is in charge of a conversation, a take-over bar sits where the message box would be and says who holds it: *"The AI handles this conversation"*, *"The AI is answering the user"*, *"Another operator is handling this conversation"* or *"This conversation is closed"*. Taking over, handing back and generating a summary are for administrators.

### Step in and reply

1. Select **Take over**. The conversation is assigned to you, the assistant pauses, and the message box appears.
2. Reply as needed. **Ctrl+Enter** (**Cmd+Enter** on macOS) sends.
3. When you're done, select **Hand back to the AI**. If you've left an unsent draft, confirm with **Discard draft and hand back**.

Only one operator can hold a conversation. If someone else already has it, there's no **Take over** button. The conversation goes back to the assistant only when you hand it back.

### Stop an answer without taking over

If the assistant is stuck on one answer, for example looping or waiting on a broken tool, select **Stop generating** in the take-over bar. The answer ends, and the assistant replies normally to the user's next message. **Stop generating** is also in the operator bar after you take over.

### Read the conversation summary

1. Select **Generate summary** in the take-over bar. The summary covers the main problem, key points and resolution status, with a status badge (**Open**, **In progress** or **Resolved**) and the conversation's sentiment.
2. Select **Copy summary** to copy it, or **Take over** to take the conversation from there.

Summaries are also listed under **Summary** in the **Details** drawer, which offers **Generate summary** too. They aren't stored, so the drawer lists only the ones generated in your current session. To turn summaries on and choose their model, see [Configure conversation processing](/operational-guides/conversation-processing#summary-generation).

## Run bulk actions

1. Select the rows you want using their checkboxes. To select a range, select the first checkbox, then hold **Shift** and select the last one.
2. Open the **Actions** menu, which appears next to the header once rows are selected and shows how many you picked.
3. Choose one of:
   - **Refresh labels**: re-run the automatic analysis (such as sentiment and topics) on the selected conversations.
   - **Add to favourites** or **Remove from favourites**.
   - **Delete**: remove the selected conversations, with their messages and attachments. The confirmation states how many conversations will be deleted.

Rows stay selected when you sort or change filters, so a deletion may include rows you can't currently see. Check the count in the confirmation.
