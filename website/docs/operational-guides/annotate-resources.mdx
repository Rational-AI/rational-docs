# Annotate resources

Highlight a passage in a text resource and give it a label and a color, or leave a comment on a PDF, so you and your colleagues find it again later. Annotations show inline in the viewer and are listed in the **Annotations** sidebar. A workflow can also create annotations for you, including on images and tables (**Auto-annotate**).

## Prerequisites

- **Rights on the Knowledge:** **Read** on the **Knowledge** row of your role to see annotations, **Create** to add them, **Update** to change them, and **Delete** to remove them. See [Manage roles and users](/operational-guides/roles-and-users).
- **A text resource** in a supported format: `.txt` or `.md`. Other formats such as `json`, `xml`, and `html` open in the syntax-highlighted editor and can't be annotated here.
- **A PDF resource**, if you want to leave comments on a document.

## Open a text resource

1. Open the **Knowledge** tab and select the card of the Knowledge that holds your resource.
2. Open the `.txt` or `.md` resource. A `.txt` file shows as selectable text. A `.md` file opens on **Preview**: select **Source** to annotate it.

Editing a Markdown source after you annotate it can shift existing highlights, because they don't move with the text.

## Create an annotation

1. In the viewer, **select the text** you want to annotate by dragging across it.
2. When you release the selection, the **label/color popover** opens.
3. Choose or enter a **label** and pick a **color**.
4. Confirm to create the annotation.

The selected passage is highlighted inline in your chosen color, and the new annotation is added to the **Annotations** sidebar with its label and color.

Annotations can't overlap or nest.

## Review and manage annotations

In the **Annotations** sidebar:

- Select an entry to scroll the viewer to that passage and flash its highlight.
- Delete an entry to remove its highlight from the text.

## Comment on a PDF

A comment attaches a note to a passage or to an area of a PDF page, where your colleagues see it.

1. Open a PDF resource in the Knowledge.
2. In the tool rail beside the document, open the small arrow on the **comment** button (*"Comment options"*) and pick a gesture. The button remembers your choice, so next time a single click is enough:
   - **Comment on the text**: select the passage you want to comment on. The selected text is kept as the quotation.
   - **Comment on an area**: draw a rectangle around a region, such as a chart or a signature, that has no selectable text.
3. In the popover, type your note in the *"Write a comment"* field.
4. Select **Save**. The comment appears on the page as a note that carries its text, and it scrolls and zooms with the document.

If you close the popover without saving, no comment is created.

### Review and edit comments

The **Annotations** panel lists comments grouped by page, each with its note and, for a text comment, the passage it refers to underneath.

- **Edit a comment:** change the note directly in the panel.
- **Jump to a comment:** select its **inspect** icon to scroll the document to it.
- **Remove a comment:** select its **delete** icon and confirm in the **Delete annotation** dialog.

### Show or hide annotations on the page

1. Select the **eye** button (*"Show or hide annotations"*).
2. Turn **Text**, **Images**, **Tables**, or **Comments** on or off, or use **Hide all** and **Show all**.

This changes only what is drawn on the document: the **Annotations** panel keeps listing everything.

## Check that chunks are ready for search

Open a resource and select the **Chunks** tab. The dot on each chunk card says whether [search](/operational-guides/configure-rag) can find it:

| Dot | What it means |
|-----|---------------|
| Green | Search can find the chunk. |
| Warning color | Search can't find the chunk yet. [Reprocess the file](/operational-guides/manage-knowledge-resources#recover-a-file-that-failed) to fix it. |
| Gray | The chunk is disabled, so search ignores it. |
