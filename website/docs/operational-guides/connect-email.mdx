# Connect a mailbox

An Email channel connects a mailbox to Rational AI. Incoming mail becomes conversations, and replies go out as email in the same thread.

A [touchpoint](/operational-guides/configure-touchpoints) whose channel is this mailbox answers the mail. An agent of [Agents](/cr/Agents) can also be bound to the mailbox, so that a new email starts it. A mailbox that no touchpoint uses and no agent is bound to is left unread.

## Prerequisites

- **A mailbox with IMAP and SMTP access**, and its server addresses.
- **Credentials**: either an app password, or a Gmail or Microsoft 365 account you can authorize.
- **Permissions:** a role with **Create** and **Update** on **Channels**.

## Connect the mailbox

1. Go to **Settings** → **Channels** and select **Add**.
2. Choose **Email** as the **Channel type**.
3. Enter a **Name** and fill in **IMAP Host**, **SMTP Host** and **Email Address**. The ports default to 993 for IMAP and 587 for SMTP, and **Transport security** to `auto`, which picks the TLS mode from the port.
4. Choose the **Authentication** method and enter the credentials (see [Choose how to authenticate](#choose-how-to-authenticate)).
5. Choose the [reply mode](/operational-guides/manage-channels#choose-the-mode). **Manual** is a safe start: mail is put in front of a person, and nothing is sent until someone replies.
6. Select **Save**.
7. In the channel's details, select **Connect**. With an app password, this checks both the IMAP and the SMTP login. With OAuth, select **Authorize mailbox access** and complete your provider's consent screen.
8. Open the touchpoint that should answer and, on its **Settings** tab, select this channel as its **Channel**. The touchpoint must be enabled.

## Choose how to authenticate

| **Authentication** | Use it for |
|--------|-----------|
| **app password** *(default)* | Any IMAP and SMTP mailbox. Enter it in **Password**. |
| **OAuth (Google)** or **OAuth (Microsoft 365)** | Gmail and Microsoft 365, where app passwords are often turned off by policy. Enter your OAuth application's **OAuth Client ID** and **OAuth Client Secret**. |

## How mail becomes conversations

Each email thread is one conversation, so two threads from the same person are two conversations, as in their mail client.

Rational AI picks up unread mail every 60 seconds by default (**Poll interval (seconds)**) and marks a message read once it's in a conversation. HTML-only mail is reduced to plain text.

Replies are sent with a `Re:` subject and the headers that keep them inside the existing thread in the recipient's mail client. If your mail server is unreachable, the reply is sent again later.

## Filter automated and unverified mail

The channel ignores mail that would start a reply loop:

- Mail marked as automatically sent, or as bulk, list or junk mail.
- Out-of-office replies and other mail that asks not to be auto-answered.
- Mail from **no-reply**, **mailer-daemon** and **postmaster** addresses.
- Mail from the channel's own address.

Your [allowed and blocked senders](/operational-guides/manage-channels#control-who-can-write-in) apply on top. On an **Automatic** mailbox, fill in **Allowed senders**: mailboxes get far more unsolicited mail than chat apps.

**Only verified senders** is on by default. Mail that fails the sender checks (DMARC, DKIM or SPF) is kept for a person to read, but it starts no agent and gets no automatic reply. Mail between colleagues on the same Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace may also count as unverified. The check needs a mail server that records these results, as Gmail and Microsoft 365 do.

## Attachments

Attachments follow the channel's [attachment settings](/operational-guides/manage-channels#control-what-can-be-sent-in). **Attachment size limit (MB)** defaults to 25 MB. The assistant reads the text of an accepted document before it answers. Operators can open or download what arrived from the conversation; see [Use the chat](/operational-guides/chat-experience).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| A mailbox that worked has stopped receiving mail | The channel's **Status**. An OAuth authorization can expire or be revoked. Select **Disconnect**, then **Connect**, and complete **Authorize mailbox access** again. You don't need to re-enter the client ID or secret. |
| **Connect** fails with an app password | Both the IMAP and the SMTP login must work. Check the hosts, ports, **Email Address** and **Password**. |
| Mail arrives but gets no automatic reply | That the **Reply mode** is **Automatic**, that an enabled touchpoint uses the channel, and whether **Only verified senders** held back mail that failed the sender checks. |
