# Review the audit trail

**Settings** → **Audit trail** records who read or changed a record in Rational AI, such as who opened a conversation or who deleted a user. Entries can't be edited once written.

The audit trail is not the [Governance](/operational-guides/governance) **Audit log**, which has one row per model request with its model, tokens and cost.

## Prerequisites

- **Administrative access:** the audit trail is restricted to administrators.

## Read the trail

Go to **Settings** → **Audit trail**. Each entry shows:

- **When** the action happened.
- **Who** did it. An entry with no person reads **System**, for background work. The name is kept as it was, so it stays correct after the user is deleted.
- **Action**: what was done.
- **Resource**: what was acted on.
- **Detail**: a one-line summary.

The list shows the newest entries first. To narrow it, filter by action: **Read**, **Created**, **Updated**, **Deleted**, **Started**, **Cancelled**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**. Deletions are highlighted in color.

A **Read** entry is written only when the access was allowed, so refused attempts don't appear as reads. Nothing in the product deletes audit entries: [data retention](/operational-guides/data-retention) periods don't apply to them.

## What is recorded

- **Reading a conversation.**
- **Deleting a user**, including a user deleting their own account.
- **Erasing a person's data**, recorded before anything is erased. See [Erase a person's data](/operational-guides/erase-personal-data).
- **Governance decisions**: registering, changing and deleting a [governed application](/operational-guides/govern-ai-applications), with any change of status or risk tier; approving a model, an approval revoked by an edit, and every change to the model approval policy.
- **Changes to [BI dashboards](/operational-guides/rational-bi) and [knowledge views](/operational-guides/knowledge-custom-views)**: every update and deletion, including changes made by the workspace assistant. The entry keeps the content before and after the change. A save that changes nothing records nothing.
- **Touchpoint memory**: adding, editing, approving, discarding and deleting a shared memory, and a user asking the assistant to forget what it remembered about them.
- **[Agents](/operational-guides/create-an-agent)**: creating, changing, pausing and deleting an agent; starting and cancelling its runs; approving or rejecting its tool calls; and the emails and messages a run sends.

Actions not in this list aren't recorded yet.

The list doesn't show the content before and after a change, because it can hold the very data the entry protects. To recover a previous version of a dashboard or a view, ask Rational AI. Each read of that content is itself recorded as a **Read** entry.
