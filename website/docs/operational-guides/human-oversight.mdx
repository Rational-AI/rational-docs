# Record oversight decisions

Human oversight rules flag the conversations a person should look at. When you have reviewed one, record your decision on the conversation, so there is a record of who looked, what they concluded, and whether they stepped in.

## Prerequisites

- **Administrator role:** only administrators can record a decision. Other roles with **Update** on **Conversations** see the review panel, but their decision is refused with *"The review could not be recorded"*.
- **Human oversight rules:** set them in **Settings** → **Conversations** → **Human oversight** (see [Conversations settings](/cr/settings/conversations#human-oversight)). Without rules, nothing is flagged.

## Find flagged conversations

Flagged conversations show **Needs review** in the [Conversations](/operational-guides/conversations) list. To list only those, open **Filters**, select **Needs review** under **Show only**, and select **Apply**.

## Record a decision

1. Open a flagged conversation. The review panel is at the end of the details drawer. If the drawer is closed, select **Needs review** in the conversation header.
2. Read the conversation and, optionally, enter the reason for your decision in **Note (optional)**.
3. Select the outcome:
   - **Leave as it is**: you reviewed it and no action was needed.
   - **I stepped in**: you reviewed it and intervened.

The flag is resolved, and the panel collapses to one line with the outcome (*"Reviewed: left to stand"* or *"Reviewed: the operator stepped in"*), the time and the note. The conversation also keeps who recorded the decision. A recorded decision can't be undone.
