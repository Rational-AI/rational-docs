# Work with records in collections and grid views

Plenty of what a team knows isn't a document: it's a list of customers, contracts, suppliers or incidents, each with a few fields that change every week. Keeping that list in a spreadsheet next to the AI means it is always out of date and the assistant can't see it. In Rational AI a [Knowledge](/operational-guides/create-knowledge) can hold those records directly: you group them into **collections**, edit them in a **grid** like a spreadsheet, save the filtered and grouped layouts your team needs as **views**, and see who changed what in each record's **History**.

:::note[What are records, collections and grid views?]
A **record** is a resource of the Knowledge described by its attributes (fields such as *Status*, *Amount* or *Renewal date*) rather than, or as well as, by a file. A **collection** is a named type of record inside one Knowledge, such as *Companies*, *Contacts* and *Opportunities*: each record belongs to at most one collection, and each collection shows its own attributes in its own order. A **grid view** is a saved way of looking at the records (which collection, which columns, which filters, groups, sorting and summaries) that everyone with access to the Knowledge can open.
:::

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- **Access to the Knowledge:** read access is enough to browse records, reshape a view for yourself and pin views.
- **Update rights to change it:** creating collections, editing records and saving views need the Knowledge **update** permission; deleting records needs the **delete** permission. Without them the grid is read-only. See [Manage roles and users](/operational-guides/roles-and-users).
- **Attributes:** the grid shows one column per attribute. You can add them from the grid itself; for the kinds available, see [Attribute kinds](/operational-guides/knowledge-attributes).

## Open the grid

1. Open the **Knowledge** tab and select the card of your Knowledge.
2. In the header, open the views panel (the button with the current view's icon and name) and choose **Grid**.

The grid has the fixed columns **Name**, **Description**, **Category** and **Tags**, then one column per attribute, then **Relations** with the number of linked resources. A new page shows 25 records; use the pagination controls at the bottom for more.

An empty Knowledge shows *"This Knowledge is empty"* with **Add manually**, **Import from Excel or CSV** and **Go to settings**. To bring in an existing spreadsheet, see [Import records from Excel or CSV](/operational-guides/import-knowledge#import-records-from-excel-or-csv).

## Organize records into collections

Collections are created and managed from the grid; there is no separate settings page for them.

1. Select **+ New collection** on the right of the toolbar (or, once collections exist, the **+** at the end of the tab bar, *"New collection"*).
2. Enter a **Collection name** (unique in the Knowledge) and select **Create**.

As soon as there is one collection, a tab bar appears above the grid: **All resources** with the total count, then one tab per collection with its own count. Both counts follow the current search and filter. Drag the tabs to reorder them. On **All resources** an extra **Collection** column shows where each record belongs; you can filter and group by it.

To move records into a collection, use **Move to collection** in a row's **⋯** menu, or select several rows and use the bulk action (see [Select records across pages](#select-records-across-pages)).

The active tab has a **⋮** menu with:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Changes the collection's name. |
| **Attributes** | Chooses which of the Knowledge's attributes the collection shows, and in what order (drag to reorder). |
| **Show in History** | Shows this collection's records in the **History** of the records they link to, placed by one of their date attributes. Useful for meetings, calls or invoices that belong on a customer's timeline. |
| **Open records with** | Opens this collection's records in one of your custom views instead of the standard drawer. See [Open a collection's records in a custom view](#open-a-collections-records-in-a-custom-view). |
| **Delete** | Deletes the collection. |

:::note[Deleting a collection keeps its records]
Deleting a collection does not delete what is in it: its records stay in the Knowledge, without a collection, under **All resources**. What goes with it are the reminders set on it and any grid view that showed only that collection.
:::

## Edit records in the grid

With update rights, the grid works like a spreadsheet.

1. Start editing a cell by double-clicking it, pressing **Enter** or **F2**, or typing. Press **Space** to tick a checkbox cell.
2. Move with the arrow keys, **Tab**, **Home** and **End**; **Delete** or **Backspace** clears a cell, **Escape** cancels the edit.
3. Add a record with **+ New row** at the bottom, or press **Shift+Enter** to insert a row below the current one. A new row is marked **New** until it is saved.
4. Edits are not written as you type: changed cells stay highlighted and a bar at the bottom counts them (*"3 unsaved changes"*). Select **Save changes**, or press **Ctrl+S** (**Cmd+S** on macOS), to write them all at once.

Each kind of attribute has its own editor: a date picker, a person picker with a **Me** shortcut, a link picker over the linked collection, and so on.

:::note[A save writes everything or nothing]
All pending edits are saved together, so a batch never lands halfway. If a value doesn't fit its attribute, the bar shows how many are invalid and **Save changes** stays disabled until you fix them. If someone else changed one of your rows after you started editing, nothing is saved and the **Rows changed since you started editing** dialog lets you **Reload rows** to see their current values; your edits to the other rows stay pending. **Discard changes** asks for confirmation before throwing your edits away, and leaving the page with pending edits asks first too.
:::

Actions that write immediately, such as renaming an attribute, moving or deleting records, importing or deleting a collection, first ask you to save or discard the edits you have pending.

### Create categories, tags and options from a cell

You don't have to prepare every option in settings first. In a **Category**, **Tags** or select cell, type in *"Search or create…"*: when the name doesn't exist yet, it is created on the spot with the next unused color, and appears as a colored chip in every record. The new option is saved to the Knowledge immediately; the cell's value still waits for **Save changes**.

### Edit relations

The **Relations** cell shows how many resources are linked to the record. Select it to open a list of those relations, where you can add or remove them without leaving the grid. For typed links between collections, use a **Link** attribute instead; see [Attribute kinds](/operational-guides/knowledge-attributes#link).

### Follow a rollup to its records

Select a cell of a **Rollup** column (*"Show these records"*) to see what it counts. When the rollup adds up the records that link *to* this one (all the opportunities of a company, say), the grid switches to their collection, filtered to those records. When it follows the record's own link, the linked record opens.

## Find records: Filter, sort, group and summarize

The toolbar has **Filter**, **Group by**, **Sort** and **Visible attributes**. Each runs on the server, so it covers every record in the Knowledge, not only the page on screen.

- **Filter:** select **Add condition** and build conditions such as *Status* **is any of** *Open*, or *Renewal date* **is within** *the next 30 days*. Combine them with **and** or **or**, and use **Add condition group** for one level of nested conditions. Operators follow the attribute kind (text, number, date, select, checkbox, link, person). On a person attribute, choose **Me**: everyone who opens the view then sees their own records.
- **Sort:** add one or more levels, each **Ascending** or **Descending**. Besides the attributes you can sort, and filter, by **Last modified**, to see what moved recently.
- **Group by:** up to three levels. Group headers show the value and a count; use **Collapse all** or **Expand all**.
- **Visible attributes:** show or hide columns (**Name** always stays).
- **Summaries:** the footer of each column has a **Summary** button: counts of empty, filled and unique values or their percentages for every column; **Sum**, **Average**, **Median**, **Min**, **Max**, **Range** and **Standard deviation** for numbers; **Earliest** and **Latest** for dates. Summaries also appear in group headers.

A filter, group or summary on an attribute that was later deleted or changed type is ignored and marked as such, and dropped the next time the view is saved.

## Save and share a grid view

The layout you build lives in the page address, so you can already send it as a link. To give it a name everyone finds again, save it as a view.

1. In the **Grid**, select **Save as view**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Under **Collection**, keep *"All resources, with the collection tabs"* or pick one collection; the view then shows that collection only, without tabs.
4. Under **Show as**, keep **Grid view** (or choose **Calendar** for a calendar of a date attribute; see [Custom views](/operational-guides/knowledge-custom-views#show-records-on-a-calendar)).
5. Select **Create**.

:::note[Saved views belong to the Knowledge, not to you]
A grid view is shared: everyone who can open the Knowledge sees it in the views panel, and only people with update rights can save one. To keep a layout to yourself, don't save it; use **Copy link** instead, or [pin](#pin-the-views-you-use-most) the shared views you need.
:::

When you change the filters, columns or grouping of a saved view, the header shows **View changed** with:

- **Copy link**: copies a link to exactly what you see, without saving it. Anyone can use it.
- **Discard**: goes back to the saved layout.
- **Save as new view** and **Save**: for people with update rights. If someone else saved the view in the meantime, it is reloaded instead of being overwritten.

Saved grid views are also listed on the Knowledge's **Views** settings tab, where you can rename, disable, delete them or choose **Set as default view**.

## Pin the views you use most

The views panel lists every view under **Pinned**, **Default** and **Views**, with a search box. Select the star beside a view, or press **Shift+Enter** on it, to pin it.

- You can pin up to **5** views per Knowledge. Once you have five, unpin one to pin another.
- Pinned views appear as tabs between the header and the view. Drag them, or press **Alt+Left** / **Alt+Right**, to reorder them.
- Pins are personal and follow you across devices; pinning needs only read access. The built-in **Graph** and **Grid** can be pinned too.

Each view shows an icon in the panel and on its tab. Views generated with AI get an icon chosen to match their content; the others use their type's icon (a table for grids, a calendar for calendars, a page for custom views).

## Select records across pages

When the Knowledge has at least one collection, each row has a checkbox for users with update rights.

1. Tick rows one by one, or the header checkbox to select the whole page.
2. To go beyond the page, use **Select all N resources**: the selection now covers every record matching the current collection, search and filter.
3. Choose a bulk action:
   - **Move to collection**: moves the selection to a collection, or to **No collection**.
   - **Delete**: deletes the selection after confirmation. Records are deleted with their links and history.

:::warning[Deleting records also deletes their links and history]
Deleted records cannot be restored from the grid. Before a large bulk delete, check the filter that defines your selection, or [export the Knowledge](/operational-guides/export-knowledge) first.
:::

## Open a record

Select the open icon at the start of a row, or **See details** in its **⋯** menu. A record opens in a drawer on the right; a file or a database resource opens in the viewer.

The drawer has the tabs **Details**, **Attributes** and **History**, plus **Annotations** and **Chunks** when the resource has them. On the **Attributes** tab, the collection's attributes come first, and attributes outside the collection that hold a value appear under **Other**. **Linked from** lists the records that point to this one.

:::note[The drawer saves as you go]
Unlike the grid, the **Attributes** tab saves each field as soon as you change it, and shows **Saved**. If the record also has pending edits in the grid, the drawer tells you, with **Discard** and **Save changes**.
:::

### Read a record's history

The **History** tab is a timeline of the record, newest first and grouped by day. Each entry says who made the change (a person, an assistant, an agent or the system), how (*via import*, *via package*, *via agent tool*), and what changed, with the value before and after. Several saves by the same person within five minutes are shown as one entry. Records of collections set to **Show in History** appear as linked cards at their date.

History is kept for 24 months, up to the latest 500 entries per record.

### Open a collection's records in a custom view

A record type sometimes deserves its own page: a contract summary, a customer profile. To use one of your [custom views](/operational-guides/knowledge-custom-views) as the record page of a collection:

1. On the collection's tab, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Open records with**.
2. Pick an HTML, JSX or Web Component view (or **Standard view** to go back to the drawer).

From then on, every record of that collection opens in that view, from the grid, from link chips or from the changed-records list. If the view is later deleted or disabled, records open in the drawer again.

## See what changed while you were working

Assistants, agents and colleagues may update records while you have the grid open. The grid checks every 30 seconds and, when something changed, shows *"N records were changed by an assistant, an agent or another person."* with **Reload**. Select the message itself to see the list of the most recent changed records, with who changed them, and open any of them. Reloading keeps your unsaved edits; a row that changed in the meantime asks before your edit overwrites it.

Rows last changed by an assistant also carry a small sparkle icon beside their name.

## Result

Your Knowledge now works as a small database: records grouped into collection tabs, edited in place and saved in one batch, found with server-side filters, groups, sorting and summaries, and kept as shared grid views that each person can pin. Every record has a drawer with its attributes and a history of who changed what, and the grid tells you when someone else has changed records under you.
