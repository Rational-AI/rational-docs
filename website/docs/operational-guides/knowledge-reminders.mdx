# Get reminded before a date in your records

Contracts expire, certificates lapse, invoices fall due, and the date is sitting right there in a column nobody looks at until it is too late. Rational AI can watch the date attributes of a [Knowledge](/operational-guides/knowledge-collections-and-grid) for you: you set a reminder once, such as *30 days before Renewal date*, and every matching record notifies the right people, in the Control Room and optionally by email, when its day comes.

:::note[What is a reminder?]
A **reminder** is a rule on one date or date-and-time attribute of a Knowledge: *when* to notify (so many days or hours before or after the date, or on the day), *who* to notify (users, roles, or the people each record names), and optionally *which records* (a collection tab and a filter). Rational AI checks every reminder throughout the day and sends one notification per record, per day, to each recipient.
:::

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- **Update rights on the Knowledge:** creating, editing and deleting reminders needs the Knowledge **update** permission.
- **A date attribute:** a **Date** or **Date and time** attribute with values in your records. Computed attributes (formulas, rollups, linked fields) can't carry a reminder. See [Attribute kinds](/operational-guides/knowledge-attributes).
- **Email, if you want it:** reminders by email need the platform's outgoing email to be configured by your administrator; without it, reminders are delivered in the Control Room only.

## Create a reminder

1. Open the Knowledge's **Grid**, and, if the reminder is about some records only, select their collection tab and set a filter (for example *Status* **is** *Active*).
2. Open the menu of the date column and choose **Remind me…**. (You can also select **Reminders** in the toolbar, then **New reminder**.)
3. Give the reminder a **Name**. It is the first thing recipients read, so make it say what is due, such as *Contract renewal*.
4. Under **Remind**, set when to notify: a number and **days before**, **on the day of** or **days after** the date attribute. On a date-and-time attribute you can also choose **hours before** or **hours after**. The default is 7 days before; the offset can be up to 366 days.
5. Keep **Only the resources the grid shows: this tab and its filter** ticked to limit the reminder to the records you selected in step 1, or untick it to cover every record of the Knowledge.
6. Under **Notify**, choose at least one recipient:
   - **Users**: specific people. You are selected by default.
   - **Roles**: everyone who has the role when the reminder fires.
   - **The people the record names**: for each record, the users named in one of its person attributes, shown as *"The record's Account manager"*. Available when the Knowledge has [person attributes](/operational-guides/knowledge-attributes#person).
7. Tick **Also send by email** if recipients should get an email as well.
8. Check the preview at the bottom: it lists how many reminders will go out in the next 30 days, and the first few records with their dates.
9. Select **Save**.

:::note[Only people who can read the Knowledge are notified]
Recipients are worked out when the reminder fires, not when you save it: a role includes whoever holds it that day, and the record's people are whoever the record names that day. Anyone who can't read the Knowledge at that moment is skipped, so a reminder never shows a record to someone who isn't allowed to see it.
:::

## Know when reminders fire

| Attribute | When the notification goes out |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | On the due day, from **8:00** in the time zone of the browser the reminder was created in. Days that have already passed are not sent: a new reminder never notifies about dates in the past. |
| **Date and time** | Within about a quarter of an hour of the due time. If the platform was unavailable at that time, the reminder still goes out up to a day late, but not later. |

Each person is notified once per record and due day, however many times the check runs. If the filter of a reminder uses **Me**, it means the person who created the reminder.

## Read your notifications

Reminders arrive in the **Notifications** bell in the Control Room header, with an unread count. Each one reads like *"Contract renewal: Acme Ltd (Renewal date 2026-12-01)"*; select it to open the record. Use the **All** and **Unread** tabs to filter the list, and **Mark all as read** to clear it.

With **Also send by email**, each recipient also gets one email per run, with the subject *"[Reminder] Contract renewal"*, listing their records with a link to each. People without an email address on their account receive the in-app notification only.

The bell also tells people when they are named in a person attribute (*"You were assigned to …"*), including once per import when an [import](/operational-guides/import-knowledge) assigns them several records.

## Manage reminders

Select **Reminders** in the grid toolbar to see every reminder of the Knowledge, with a summary such as *"30 days before Renewal date · Sales, The record's Account manager"*.

- Select **Edit** to change a reminder. To pause it without losing it, untick **Enabled**: it shows as *paused* in the list.
- Select **Delete** to remove it. Notifications it has already sent stay where they are.

:::note[Renames are followed, deletions are not]
If you rename the date attribute, or a person attribute the reminder notifies, the reminder follows the new name. If you delete the attribute, or change it to a kind that isn't a date, the reminder stays in the list but stops sending, so check it after reorganizing your attributes. Deleting a collection deletes the reminders set on it.
:::
