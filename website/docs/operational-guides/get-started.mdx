# Get started with Rational AI

Rational AI is an AI platform that runs on your own servers. You and your colleagues use it from the AI Control Room, a web app where you chat with AI assistants, search your company's documents, and build dashboards. This page gets you to a first answer, then shows a builder how to set up an assistant that answers from your own content.

## Start chatting

Every signed-in user can use **Chat**, and it is the page the Control Room opens on.

1. Sign in to the Control Room.
2. Type a question in the message box and send it. The answer comes from **Rational Chat**, the workspace assistant.
3. To talk to a different assistant, select the assistant's name at the top of the page and pick one of the touchpoints you can use.

## Build an assistant on your own content

Builders set up the assistants that everyone else chats with. An assistant answers from your content through RAG (retrieval-augmented generation): it finds the most relevant passages in your documents and writes its answer from them. See [RAG](/concepts/RAG) for a short introduction.

You work with four building blocks, in this order. A **model** generates the text, and you add it in the [AI Model Registry](/operational-guides/ai-model-registry-features). A **Knowledge** holds your content and the rules for searching it. A **touchpoint** combines a model, a Knowledge, and tools into an assistant, and a [channel](/operational-guides/manage-channels) makes a touchpoint available to your customers outside the Control Room.

### Prerequisites

- **The Knowledge module in your license.** Without it, the **Knowledge** tab doesn't appear.
- **An Admin account, or a role with Create** on the **AI Model Registry** and **Knowledge** rows. See [Manage roles and users](/operational-guides/roles-and-users).
- **Some content to load**: documents, an FAQ, a spreadsheet, anything text-based.

### 1. Connect a model

Your assistant needs two kinds of model: a **text-generation** model to write answers, and an **embedding** model to turn your content into something searchable.

1. Go to **Settings** → **AI Model Registry** and select **New**.
2. Pick how to add the model:
   - **Choose an open source model** runs a model from Hugging Face on your own servers.
   - **Add a proxy to a third-party model** uses a provider such as OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic with your API key. The key is stored in a [connector](/operational-guides/configure-connectors).
3. Confirm that one text-generation model and one embedding model show as **Available**.

See [Manage AI models in the registry](/operational-guides/ai-model-registry-features) for every option.

### 2. Create a Knowledge

1. Open the **Knowledge** tab and select **New** → **New knowledge**. Rational AI creates it with a default name and opens its settings, where you give it a clear name.
2. On the **AI Search** tab, turn on **Allow AI search**, pick your **Embedding model**, and check the **Chunking type** (the default is **Recursive Chunker**).
3. Select **Save**.

Creating a Knowledge also creates a touchpoint named after it (for example *Sales FAQ - Touchpoint* for a Knowledge called *Sales FAQ*), with the tools to search it. See [Create a Knowledge](/operational-guides/create-knowledge).

### 3. Add your data

A new Knowledge is empty. Fill it in one of two ways:

- **Upload files** into the Knowledge. They are split into chunks and embedded straight away. See [Add and manage content](/operational-guides/manage-knowledge-resources).
- **Connect a source**, such as a shared drive, a website, or a database. Its files are processed when the source syncs, according to its sync mode (**Manual**, **Periodic** or **Automatic**). See [Connect Google Drive](/operational-guides/integrate-data).

### 4. Chat with your assistant

1. Open the **Knowledge** tab and select your Knowledge.
2. Select **Chat**. The chat starts on the Knowledge's touchpoint.
3. Ask a question you already know the answer to, and check that the reply matches your content.

Your colleagues reach the same assistant from **Chat** by picking the Knowledge's touchpoint at the top of the page. To change its model, system prompt, or tools, open **Settings** → **Touchpoints**; see [Configure touchpoints](/operational-guides/configure-touchpoints).
