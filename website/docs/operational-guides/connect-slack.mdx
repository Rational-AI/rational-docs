A Slack channel lets people chat with a [touchpoint](/operational-guides/configure-touchpoints) in Slack: in a direct message to your app, or by mentioning it in a Slack channel. Each exchange appears in [Conversations](/cr/Conversations) like any other.

## Before you begin

- **Role:** a role with the **Create** and **Update** actions on the **Channels** row (see [Roles and users](/operational-guides/roles-and-users)). **Update** is needed to connect the channel and to change it later.
- **A public HTTPS address for Rational AI.** Slack delivers events to your deployment over the internet, so the address Rational AI shows for the channel must be reachable from outside your network.
- **A Slack app in your workspace with a bot user**, and permission to install it. Give the bot these scopes on the app's **OAuth & Permissions** page:
  - `app_mentions:read`
  - `chat:write`
  - `im:history`
  - `im:read`
- **A touchpoint to answer**, or the plan to create one. The channel only carries messages; the touchpoint holds the prompt, knowledge, and tools.

## Create the channel

1. In Slack, install the app to your workspace and copy two values:
   - The **Bot User OAuth Token** (it starts with `xoxb-`), from **OAuth & Permissions**.
   - The **Signing Secret**, from **Basic Information**.
2. In Rational AI, go to **Settings** → **Channels** and select **Add**.
3. In **Channel type**, select **Slack**.
4. Enter a **Name**. It must be unique across your channels.
5. Under **Configuration**, enter the **Bot token** and the **Signing secret**. Both are required.
6. Optional: fill in **Allowed senders** and **Blocked senders** (see [Control who can write in](#control-who-can-write-in)), and set **Split replies** to **Yes** to send a reply as a few short messages.
7. Select a **Reply mode**: **Automatic** lets the assistant answer on its own; **Manual** and **Draft only** keep a person in charge of every reply. See [Choose the mode](/operational-guides/manage-channels#choose-the-mode).
8. Select **Save**.

## Subscribe the Slack app to events

1. In **Settings** → **Channels**, open the channel you created and copy the **Request URL** from the **Connection** section.
2. In the Slack app's settings, open **Event Subscriptions**, turn on events, and paste the address as the **Request URL**. Slack checks it straight away; this works before you connect the channel, as long as the **Signing secret** is saved.
3. Under the bot events, subscribe to `app_mention` and `message.im`, and save.
4. If people should write to the app in direct messages, allow messages in the app's **App Home** settings in Slack.

## Connect the channel

1. In the channel's details, select **Connect**. Rational AI checks the bot token with Slack and records the workspace it belongs to.
2. Check that the details show **Connected**.
3. Open the touchpoint that should answer and, in its **Settings** tab, select this channel as its **Channel**. The touchpoint must be enabled. If several enabled touchpoints use the channel, the one created first answers.
4. Invite the app to each Slack channel where people should mention it.

Slack messages reach the assistant only while the channel is connected. **Disconnect** stops them; **Reconnect** checks the token again. Events from any workspace other than the one recorded at connection are refused, so select **Reconnect** after you change the **Bot token**.

## How Slack messages are handled

- **Direct messages:** the assistant answers every message. Each direct-message conversation with the app is one conversation in Rational AI.
- **Slack channels:** the assistant answers only messages that mention the app. A mention starts a thread, the reply goes into that thread, and each thread is its own conversation. The assistant never answers at the top level of a channel.
- **Ignored messages:** edited messages, messages from bots (the app itself included), and other special message types.
- **People appear by Slack user ID.** The app doesn't ask for the `users:read` scope, so conversations are named after the Slack user or channel ID, for example `Slack DM U024BE7LH`.
- **Replies** are converted to Slack formatting: bold, italic, strikethrough, code, links and quotes; headings become bold and list items become bullets.
- **No duplicates.** If Slack delivers the same event again, the assistant answers it once.

## Control who can write in

Leave **Allowed senders** empty to answer everyone who can reach the app. To restrict it, list Slack user IDs or channel IDs (for example `U024BE7LH, C0LAN2Q65`), one per line or comma-separated. **Blocked senders** takes the same IDs and wins over the allow list.

You can also change both lists later from the channel's details, under **Reply filter**, and select **Save reply filter**.

## What the channel doesn't support

- **Text only.** The assistant reads the text of a message. Files and images aren't passed to it, and a direct message sent with a file attached gets no answer. The channel has no attachment settings.
- **Text replies only.** The assistant can't send files back.
- **No reply delay.** Each message gets its own answer; the **Reply delay (seconds)** setting isn't offered for Slack.
- **No test message.** The **Test** action in the channel's details isn't available for Slack. Send the app a message from Slack instead.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Error while connecting the channel** after **Connect** | Slack refused the bot token. Copy the **Bot User OAuth Token** again (reinstalling the app can change it), select **Edit configuration**, enter it as **Bot token**, save, and select **Connect** again. |
| Slack can't verify the **Request URL** | Check that the address is exactly the **Request URL** shown in the channel's details, that Rational AI is reachable from the internet over HTTPS, and that the **Signing secret** saved in the channel matches the one on the app's **Basic Information** page. |
| A mention in a Slack channel gets no answer | Check that the app is a member of that channel, that the app is subscribed to `app_mention`, and that the sender isn't filtered out by **Reply filter**. |
| Messages arrive in Conversations but nobody answers | Check that the channel shows **Connected**, that an enabled touchpoint uses this channel, and that the **Reply mode** is **Automatic**. |
