# Erase a person's data

When someone exercises a right to erasure, closing their account isn't enough: their conversations and their identifiers on recorded model calls must go too. **Erase all data** removes them along with the account.

:::danger[Irreversible]
Erasure permanently deletes the person's conversations. There is no undo. Make sure the request is genuine and that you have the right person before you run it.
:::

## Prerequisites

- The **Delete** permission on **Users** in your [role](/operational-guides/roles-and-users), or an administrator account. Whoever can delete a user can also erase their data.
- **A verified request.** Confirming that the requester is who they say they are is part of your own process.
- **Anything you must keep, already exported.** Erasure doesn't check your legal obligations.

## Choose between Delete and Erase

The row menu on **Settings** → **Users** has two ways to remove someone:

| | **Delete user** | **Erase all data** |
|---|---|---|
| **Use it when** | Someone leaves the organization | Someone exercises a right to erasure |
| **The account** | Removed | Removed |
| **Their conversations** | Kept | Deleted, with their Canvas artifacts |
| **Their identifiers on recorded model calls** | Kept | Cleared |
| **Can be undone** | No, but you can invite the same email again | No |

Both options also remove the account from your identity provider, unless another workspace still uses the same sign-in. Neither can remove the last administrator.

## Erase a person's data

1. Go to **Settings** → **Users** and find the person.
2. Open the row's menu and select **Erase all data**.
3. Read the confirmation. It lists what is removed: their conversations, their identifier on recorded model calls, and the account.
4. Confirm.

When it finishes, a message reports how many conversations were deleted and how many recorded calls were cleared. Check that the numbers match what you expected.

## What stays

Recorded model calls keep their model, tokens, cost and timing, without the person's identifier, so your usage and cost reports stay complete.

The erasure is written to the [audit trail](/operational-guides/audit-trail) before anything is erased, and that entry names the person. It stays as evidence that you complied.

## If the identity provider is unreachable

If the identity provider can't be reached, the request fails and nothing changes in Rational AI or in the provider. Run it again once the provider is back. An account already removed from the provider counts as removed.
