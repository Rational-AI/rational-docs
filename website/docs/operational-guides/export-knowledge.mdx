# Export a Knowledge

Exporting bundles a [Knowledge](/operational-guides/create-knowledge) — its resources and configuration — into a single `.zip` archive you can download. This guide covers exporting from a Knowledge's detail page in **Settings** → **Knowledge**.

<Admonition type="info" icon="📖" title="WHEN TO EXPORT">
An export serializes everything in a Knowledge into one downloadable archive. Reach for it when you want to move a Knowledge between environments (staging → production, for example), keep a backup, or hand a curated Knowledge to another tenant. To bring an archive back in, see [Import a Knowledge](/operational-guides/import-knowledge).
</Admonition>

## Prerequisites

- **Administrative access** — an account with Knowledge management privileges.
- **An existing Knowledge** — the one you want to export must already exist. If you don't have one yet, see [Create a Knowledge](/operational-guides/create-knowledge).

## Export a Knowledge

Export when you need a portable copy of a Knowledge to move, back up, or share.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge** and open the Knowledge you want to export.
2. Open the **kebab menu** (the three-dots icon) on the detail page.
3. Select **Export**.

Rational AI queues an export job that serializes the Knowledge into a `.zip` archive on the server.

<Admonition type="note" icon="📝" title="EXPORT RUNS IN THE BACKGROUND">
Export is an asynchronous job: Rational AI prepares the archive, watches the job until it's ready, then downloads it for you. You don't need to refresh the page or start the download yourself.
</Admonition>

## Result

While the job runs, you'll see a progress toast: *"Preparing export, the download will start automatically when it's ready…"*. As soon as the archive is ready, it downloads on its own and a confirmation toast (*"Export downloaded"*) appears. If the job fails, an error toast explains what went wrong.

The downloaded `.zip` is a complete export archive. Bring it into another environment with [Import a Knowledge](/operational-guides/import-knowledge).
