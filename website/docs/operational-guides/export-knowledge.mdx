# Export and import a Knowledge

Export a Knowledge to a `.zip` archive with its resources and configuration, then import the archive to recreate the Knowledge somewhere else. Use it to move a Knowledge from a test environment to production, to keep a backup, or to hand a ready-made Knowledge to another workspace.

To bring spreadsheet rows into an existing Knowledge as records, see [Import records from Excel or CSV](/operational-guides/import-knowledge) instead.

## Prerequisites

- To export: **List** and **Read** on the **Knowledge** row of your role.
- To import: **Create** on the **Knowledge** row. Without it, the **New** menu that holds **Import knowledge** doesn't appear.

See [Manage roles and users](/operational-guides/roles-and-users).

## Export a Knowledge

1. Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge** and open the Knowledge you want to export.
2. Open the **⋮** menu beside the Knowledge's name and select **Export**.

While Rational AI prepares the archive, a toast reads *"Preparing export, the download will start automatically when it's ready…"*. Keep the page open: the archive downloads by itself and the **Export downloaded** toast appears. If the export fails, or takes longer than the 2 minutes the page waits, the **Error while exporting knowledge** toast appears instead.

## Import a Knowledge

1. Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge**, or open the **Knowledge** tab. Both have the same **New** menu, also on an empty page.
2. Select **New** → **Import knowledge**.
3. Choose the `.zip` archive from your computer.

When the import finishes, the **Knowledge imported** toast appears and Rational AI opens the new Knowledge's settings. If the archive is invalid or the upload fails, the **Error while importing knowledge** toast appears instead.

## Check the imported Knowledge

The imported Knowledge keeps the resources and configuration captured in the archive, including its default view. Before you put it to use, review its **AI Search** and **Sources** settings and its touchpoint: values such as AI models can differ from one environment to another.
