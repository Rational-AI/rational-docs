# Model architecture

A model's **architecture** is its design: how its parts are arranged and how text flows through them. Architecture decides what kind of work a model is built for, the way a floor plan decides whether a building is a warehouse or a home.

Almost every language model today uses the same design, called the **transformer**. Its main idea, *attention*, lets the model weigh each word of the input against all the others, so it keeps track of what "it" or "the contract" refers to across a long passage.

## Two kinds of model

| | Text-generation model | Embedding model |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| What it does | Writes text, one [token](/concepts/Tokens) at a time | Turns a passage into a list of numbers that captures its meaning |
| Used for | Answers in Chat and touchpoints | Making a Knowledge searchable |

## In Rational AI

**Settings** → **AI Model Registry** lists each model with its purpose, such as **Text gen**, **Embeddings**, or **Reranking**. Under **Default models**, the **Conversational model** writes answers and the **Embedding model** makes your [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) searchable. To choose a model, you don't need its internals: look at its purpose, its [context window](/concepts/Context-window), its speed, and its cost. See [AI Model Registry](/cr/settings/ai-model-registry).
