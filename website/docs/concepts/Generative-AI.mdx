# Generative AI

**Generative AI** is AI that produces new content, such as text, images or code, in response to a request. You describe what you want in plain words, and the model writes or draws it.

Older AI systems mostly sort or score things that already exist. A spam filter decides whether an email is spam; a generative model writes the reply to it.

## What it's good at

Generative AI drafts, summarizes, translates, rewrites, and answers questions in natural language. It writes the most likely continuation of your request, so the result reads well even when a fact in it is wrong. Check figures, names and sources before you use them, and see [Hallucinations](/concepts/hallucinations).

## In Rational AI

Every model you chat with in Rational AI is generative. [Chat](/cr/Chats) writes answers, and [Gen](/cr/Gen) produces images and documents. So that answers come from your own content and not only from what the model learned in training, assistants search your [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) first. See [RAG](/concepts/RAG) and [Large language models](/concepts/LLMs).
