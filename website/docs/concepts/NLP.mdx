# Natural language processing (NLP)

**Natural language processing (NLP)** is the field of AI that deals with human language: understanding it, translating it, summarizing it, and writing it.

Early NLP systems followed hand-written rules and handled one language and one task at a time. Modern NLP learns patterns from very large amounts of text, so one model can handle many tasks in many languages. [Large language models](/concepts/LLMs) are the current result of that work.

## How a computer reads text

A model can't work with words directly. It first splits text into [tokens](/concepts/Tokens), then turns them into numbers that capture meaning, so that "invoice" and "bill" end up close together. Those numbers are called [embeddings](/concepts/Embeddings-chunks-and-annotations).

## In Rational AI

NLP is behind most of what Rational AI does with text. [Chat](/cr/Chats) answers questions in plain language, a [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) finds the passage that matches a question even when it uses different words, and [Conversations](/cr/Conversations) labels conversations with their topics and sentiment.
