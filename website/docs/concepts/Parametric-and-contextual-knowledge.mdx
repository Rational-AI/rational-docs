# Parametric and contextual knowledge

A model answers from two kinds of knowledge.

- **Parametric knowledge** is what the model learned in [training](/concepts/Model-training) and carries inside it. It is broad and always there, but it stops at the model's [knowledge cutoff](/concepts/Knowledge-cutoff), knows nothing about your organization, and can't point to a source.
- **Contextual knowledge** is what the model reads in its [context window](/concepts/Context-window) for this request: the documents, records, and conversation you give it. It is current and specific, and you can check where it came from, but only what fits in the window counts.

To change parametric knowledge, you [fine-tune](/concepts/Finetuning) the model. To change contextual knowledge, you change what goes into the request.

## In Rational AI

A [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) turns your content into contextual knowledge. [RAG](/concepts/RAG) retrieves the passages that match a question and puts them in the context window, so the assistant answers from your current material and Chat shows the sources it used. The [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) page compares a model's built-in knowledge with a Knowledge side by side.
