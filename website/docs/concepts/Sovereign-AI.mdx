# Sovereign AI

**Sovereign AI** means keeping control of the AI your organization relies on: your data, the models, and where they run. You can say where your data lives, who can see it, and which provider, if any, processes it.

## In Rational AI

- **Your own machine.** An [on-premises installation](/operational-guides/on-premises-installation) runs the whole platform on a machine in your network, and your data stays on it.
- **Your own models.** Models can run on that machine's hardware. See [Run models on your own servers](/operational-guides/run-models-on-premise).
- **Providers only when you choose.** External model providers are reached only through [connectors](./Connectors.mdx) that an administrator sets up.
- **A record of every call.** [Governance](../cr/Governance.mdx) records model usage and cost, and the audit log lists each request.
- **What leaves the machine.** An on-premises installation reports its health and license use to Rational AI, never your content. See [What an installation sends to Rational AI](/operational-guides/data-sent-to-rational-ai).
