# Non-determinism

**Non-determinism** means the same question can get different answers. A model picks each word with a degree of chance from the likely options, so two runs with the same input can differ.

A setting called **temperature** controls how much. A low temperature keeps the model on its most likely choices and makes answers more repeatable; a higher one makes them more varied. Even at a low temperature, identical answers aren't guaranteed.

Two different answers can both be right. Judge each answer on whether it's correct, not on whether it matches the last one.

## In Rational AI

- **Temperature** and **Seed** are among a model's **Inference defaults**, on its page in **Settings** → **AI Model Registry**.
- An assistant that answers from your [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) keeps the facts consistent even when the wording changes. See [RAG](/concepts/RAG).
- In Chat, you can ask for another version of an answer. See [Generate an alternative answer](/operational-guides/chat-experience#generate-an-alternative-answer).
