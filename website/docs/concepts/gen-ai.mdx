# Generative AI

**Generative AI** (GenAI) is a class of artificial intelligence systems designed to **create new content**: text, images, code, audio, and more. Rather than just analyzing or classifying existing data, generative AI produces original outputs based on patterns learned from large datasets.

At its core, it is powered by deep learning models trained on vast amounts of data. These models learn the statistical structure of language, images, and other formats, and use that knowledge to generate new outputs when prompted.

## How is it different from traditional AI?

Most AI you interact with day-to-day is **traditional AI**: it analyzes existing data and makes predictions or classifications. Spam filters, product recommendations, fraud detection systems. These are all examples of traditional AI performing a specific, predefined job.

**Generative AI goes further.** Instead of labeling or sorting information, it creates something new. It can handle open-ended tasks and produce personalized outputs for situations it has never encountered before.

| Aspect       | Traditional AI        | Generative AI               |
| :----------- | :-------------------- | :-------------------------- |
| Output       | Predictions           | Content creation            |
| Scope        | Narrow, task-specific | Broad, general-purpose      |
| Data         | Mostly structured     | Structured and unstructured |
| Adaptability | Limited               | High                        |
| Interaction  | Indirect              | Natural language            |

## How does it work?

During training, a generative AI model processes large volumes of data to learn how information is typically structured. When prompted, it generates a response by estimating the most probable and relevant continuation based on those learned patterns.

This is why the quality of your prompt matters: the clearer and more specific your input, the more useful the output.

### What generative AI can do

- Write articles, emails, or documentation from a brief description

- Generate images or designs based on text prompts

- Create code for applications and automations

- Draft responses to customer inquiries

- Suggest multiple solutions to complex problems

## Limitations to keep in mind

Generative AI is powerful, but not perfect. A few things to be aware of:

- **Accuracy:** AI can make mistakes or present outdated information as fact. *Always* verify important details.

- **Context:** without clear guidance, AI may miss nuanced requirements or misinterpret your intent.

- **Originality:** AI generates based on patterns in its training data. Its outputs are novel combinations, not truly original ideas in the human sense.

- **Bias:** models can reflect biases present in the data they were trained on. Use them thoughtfully, especially for sensitive topics.
