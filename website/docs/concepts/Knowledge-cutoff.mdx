# Knowledge cutoff

A model's **knowledge cutoff** is the date its training data ends. The model knows nothing that happened, changed or was published after it, unless you give it that information.

A model rarely says that a question is past its cutoff. Asked about a new regulation or last month's price list, it often answers anyway, from older information or a plausible guess, with its usual confidence. Treat answers about recent or fast-changing facts with care.

## In Rational AI

- Your [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) holds current content, kept in sync by its [sources](/concepts/Source-and-resources), and [RAG](/concepts/RAG) gives the matching passages to the model. Answers about your organization don't depend on the cutoff.
- In Chat, **Web search** in the tools menu (**+**) lets the workspace assistant look up recent public information. See [Shape your request in the composer](/operational-guides/chat-experience#shape-your-request-in-the-composer).

For the difference between what a model learned and what it reads, see [Parametric and contextual knowledge](/concepts/Parametric-and-contextual-knowledge).
