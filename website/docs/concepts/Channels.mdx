# Channels

A **channel** is the medium through which people reach an assistant: the surface where a conversation happens. A Telegram bot, a mailbox, a Microsoft Teams or Slack bot, the web chat and the OpenAI-compatible API are all channels. The channel carries no prompt, knowledge, or tools. It changes *where* and *how* people talk to the assistant, not *what* the assistant knows.

## Why channels matter

Keeping the channel separate from the assistant means:

* **Reach:** you can meet people where they already are: the messaging app or mailbox they use.
* **Reuse:** the connection to a surface is set up once, with its own credentials, sender filters and reply mode, and any touchpoint can be put on it.
* **Fit:** each channel adapts the conversation to its surface, for example by splitting replies into short messages on a chat app.

## Channels and touchpoints

A [touchpoint](./Touchpoint.mdx) is the assistant: its models, system prompt, sources, tools, and memory. Each touchpoint has exactly one channel, chosen in its settings. To offer the same assistant on two surfaces, create a touchpoint for each channel.

Several touchpoints can share a channel, but inbound messages on that channel go to the oldest enabled touchpoint among them. An Email or messaging channel can also be bound to an agent in [Agents](../cr/Agents.mdx), which then sends messages through it.

## Channels in Rational AI

Telegram, Email, Microsoft Teams and Slack are native channel types you create in **Settings** → **Channels**. Installed extensions can add more types. The web chat and the OpenAI-compatible API are built in, and you can't create another one. See [Manage channels](../operational-guides/manage-channels.mdx).
