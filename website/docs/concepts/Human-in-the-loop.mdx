# Human-in-the-loop

**Human-in-the-loop** is a way of working in which the agent proposes and a person approves before anything consequential happens. Instead of letting the model act on its own, a human stays in the decision path, reviewing, correcting or signing off on its output.

The reason is the nature of the model itself. Output is [non-deterministic](/concepts/Non-determinism), can be confidently wrong (a [hallucination](/concepts/hallucinations)) and can drift toward telling you what you want to hear ([sycophancy](/concepts/Sycophancy)). A human in the loop is the check that catches the cases automated grounding and review do not.

:::note[A dial, not a switch]
Human-in-the-loop is not all-or-nothing. The right amount of oversight depends on the stakes: low-risk, reversible tasks may need little or none, while decisions that are costly, public or hard to undo warrant a human signing off every time.
:::

## When to keep a human in the loop

* **High stakes:** outputs that are costly, public-facing or hard to reverse.
* **Low confidence:** answers about recent or fast-changing facts, or topics near the model's [knowledge cutoff](/concepts/Knowledge-cutoff).
* **Sensitive judgement:** decisions that need accountability or domain expertise a model cannot supply.

## Human-in-the-loop in Rational AI

Grounding answers in your [Knowledge](/concepts/Knowledge) through [RAG](/concepts/RAG) narrows how far output can drift. Beyond that, a person can step in at several points:

* **Tool calls in chat:** each [touchpoint](/concepts/Touchpoint) decides when the assistant asks the user to confirm a tool before running it: never, only for sensitive tools, or always. See [Approve tool calls before they run](/operational-guides/approve-tool-calls).
* **Agent actions:** tools under **Ask me first** on an agent's profile pause the run until someone approves or rejects the call. See [Follow runs and approve actions](/operational-guides/agent-runs#approve-what-the-agent-wants-to-do).
* **Conversations:** staff can review every touchpoint conversation in **Conversations** and take one over, which suspends the AI replies. Human oversight rules flag the conversations that need a person. See [Review flagged conversations](/operational-guides/human-oversight).
