# Knowledge

![](/img/screens/cr>knowledge.png)

**Rational** **Knowledge** is where you store, organize, and manage the information your AI works with. Ingest documents and data sources, structure them into a searchable **knowledge base**, and explore relationships between resources so your models always have the right context.

Every knowledge base is a collection of related resources (documents, product catalogs, structured data) organized into two built-in views: an **interactive graph** and a **searchable table**. Additional views can be configured to match your team's workflow.

## Navigate your knowledge sets

Open the **Knowledge** section from the top navigation bar of the Control Room.  From the main screen you can:

- Search and browse existing knowledge bases**,** each showing its name and resource count;
- Create a new one by selecting **New** and providing a name and description.

Each knowledge base supports its own **categories** and **tags**, which you define to keep resources organized across your team.

## Views

Once inside a knowledge base, two views are available. You can switch between views using the toggle in the header.

The **Graph view** shows an interactive network of resources and their relationships. You can pan, zoom, re‑layout the graph or filter by tags.  Use this view to understand how pieces of your data connect.

The **Table view** shows a paginated list of resources with columns for name, tags, category, and related resource count. A search box and tag filters help you locate items quickly. &#x20;

From either view, expand any resource to open its detail panel. There you can view and edit metadata, review **annotations**, and inspect or adjust the **chunks** generated during ingestion (the text segments the model will actually read). You can also download the original file or delete the resource from here.

## Adding resources

Resources can be added to a knowledge base in two ways.

**Manually**, from the **Files** tab. Open the Files tab inside your knowledge base, click **Actions** and select **Add file**. Drop your files into the upload area, or use **Add a folder** / **Add a file** to browse your computer, then click **Upload**. In the **Review files** dialog, select a processing workflow for each file or skip processing entirely. Files are added as resources by default. Click **Confirm** to complete the upload.

**Automatically**, from connected **sources**. If you have configured data sources in the settings, resources are imported automatically as new data becomes available. See [How to integrate data](<../operational-guides/integrate data.mdx>) for a step-by-step guide on connecting external sources.

## Best practices

- **Organize by concept.** Create separate knowledge bases for distinct domains (e.g. product catalogues vs. customer support documents) to keep data manageable.
- **Use consistent naming.** Define a shared glossary for your team. Descriptive, uniform tags and categories make resources easy to find and reduce duplication.
- **Review chunks after ingestion**.  Check the generated segments in the detail painelò. Adjust chunk size and boundaries to ensure meaningful context for the models you plan to use.
- **Link related documents.** When two resources related (e.g., a purchase order and its invoce), connect them. Rich relationhips improve the context quality your models can draw on.
