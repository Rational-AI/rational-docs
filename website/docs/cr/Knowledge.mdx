# Knowledge

**Knowledge** is where you store and maintain the information Rational AI answers from. A Knowledge holds resources (files from uploads and sources, such as product catalogs) and records (rows of attributes grouped into collections), and shows them as a graph, a grid, and saved views. Builders use it to bring in data, link related resources and make the content searchable for the assistants that answer from it.

To open **Knowledge**, your license must include the Knowledge module and your role needs **Read** on the **Knowledge** row. Creating, changing and deleting need **Create**, **Update** and **Delete** on the same row. See [Manage roles and users](/operational-guides/roles-and-users).

## Find a Knowledge

Select **Knowledge** in the top navigation bar. Each card shows a Knowledge's name, its tags, a resource count and when it was last updated. Use the search bar to filter by name, and **Tags** to filter by tag.

To create one, select **New** → **New knowledge**. It is created with a default name, and its settings open so you can name and describe it.

## Switch views

Inside a Knowledge, the header shows its name, a views button with the current view's icon and name, and a **Files** button for the input files. Open the views panel to switch between:

* **Graph**: an interactive network of resources and their relationships. Use **Filters** to show one collection, filter by categories, tags, relations or sources, cluster nodes by category, tag or attribute, and color them by a number. See [Explore a Knowledge as a graph](/operational-guides/knowledge-graph).
* **Grid**: a table of records, with one tab per collection, editable cells, and filters, groups, sorting and summaries. See [Collections and grid views](/operational-guides/knowledge-collections-and-grid).
* **Saved and custom views**: grid, graph and calendar views saved by your team, dashboards, and views written in code. Pin up to five of them as tabs under the header. See [Custom views](/operational-guides/knowledge-custom-views).

## Chat with a Knowledge

A Knowledge's page has a chat panel at its side (on narrow screens, select **Chat** to open it). Select **New chat** to start a conversation. The chat starts on the Knowledge's own touchpoint and lists only the conversations held with it. Every question you send also carries the filters you applied (categories, tags, relations) and the nodes you selected in the graph.

The panel has the same conversation view as **Chat** (the tab in the top navigation), with **Advanced mode**, **Skills**, Canvas and the reasoning level. It doesn't offer **Knowledge bases**, **Web search**, **Deep Research**, **Voice conversation**, the model list or **Memory**, which belong to the workspace assistant in **Chat**. See [Chat and the other chats](/operational-guides/chat-experience#chat-and-the-other-chats).

## Work with resources

In the grid, each row is a resource or record. To see more about an item:
 
1. **Open the details drawer** with the open icon at the start of the row, or by selecting **See details** from the row's options menu. The drawer contains:

   * **Details tab:** description, category, tags, original file, last sync time and counts of incoming and outgoing relations. Use **Edit** to update the metadata, or **Open in viewer** to see the original file.

   * **Annotations tab:** review or add annotations, such as named entities, sentiment or custom notes.

   * **Attributes tab:** the record's attributes, saved as you edit them, and the records that link to it under **Linked from**.

   * **History tab:** a timeline of who changed what in the record, with the values before and after.

   * **Chunks tab:** the chunks created during ingestion. A chunk is a segment of the resource's text that search returns to the model. Each card shows the chunk number, its length, its text and its keywords. Edit a chunk's text and keywords, or delete it. Select chunks (or **Select all**) to **Enable selected**, **Disable selected** or **Delete selected**.

2. **Download or delete:** use the row's options menu to **Download** the original file or, if your role can delete, **Delete** the record.

3. **Navigate relationships:** the **Relations** column shows how many other resources are linked to this one. Select the number to see, add or remove those relations without leaving the grid.


## Add new resources

To add files to a Knowledge:
 
1. Select **Add file** in the grid's **Actions** menu (or the **Options** menu of other views).

2. Drag and drop files into the **Add file** dialog, or use **Add a folder** / **Add a file** to select items from your computer.

3. Select **Upload**. Unless the Knowledge has a processing rule, **Workflows review** opens: pick a workflow for each file, or leave **Skip workflow** and select **Add as resource**, then select **Confirm**. When processing completes, your new resources appear in the grid and graph. Tags and categories can be edited in the details drawer or directly in the grid.

To add records rather than files, type them into the grid with **+ New row**, or bring in a spreadsheet with **Import from Excel or CSV** (see [Import records from Excel or CSV](/operational-guides/import-knowledge#import-records-from-excel-or-csv)). To be notified ahead of the dates in your records, set [reminders](/operational-guides/knowledge-reminders).


## Best practices

* **Create one Knowledge per domain**, for example one for product catalogs and one for customer support documents.
* **Review the chunks** after ingestion. If the segments don't make sense, change the **Chunking type** or its options in the Knowledge's **AI Search** settings.
* **Link related resources**, such as a purchase order and its invoice, so the assistant can follow the relation when it answers.
