# Conversations

The **Conversations** module is where every exchange between your users and Rational AI's agents comes together. Whenever someone interacts with a [touchpoint](./settings/touchpoints.mdx), the messages are captured here as a single conversation and automatically enriched with a title, topics and sentiment. Use this module to monitor activity, review what your agents are saying, step into a live chat when a user needs a human, and audit individual messages down to the raw tool calls.

## Navigate your conversations

Open the **Conversations** section from the top navigation bar of the Control Room. The page presents a paginated table in which each row is one conversation. From here you can:

* **Search** across conversations using the search box in the top‑right corner.
* **Filter** the table from the **Filters** panel, and **sort** it by clicking a column header.
* **Open a conversation** to read the full thread and take action.
* **Select rows** with their checkboxes to run bulk **Actions**. Hold **Shift** and click a second checkbox to select every row in between.

The selection survives sorting and filter changes, so the bulk **Delete** confirmation always states exactly how many conversations it removes.

## The conversations table

Each row summarizes a single conversation across the following columns:

| Column                     | Description |
| :------------------------- | :---------- |
| **Conversation**           | The conversation title and a preview of the most recent message. Icons beside the title flag a conversation that **Needs review** or is **With an operator**. |
| **User and touchpoint**    | The end user who took part in the conversation, and the [touchpoint](./settings/touchpoints.mdx) it belongs to. |
| **Topics**                 | The topics detected for the conversation (for example, *Billing Request* or *Tech Support*). A conversation can carry several topics; the first two are shown as chips and the rest behind a **+N** chip. |
| **Sentiment**              | The estimated emotional tone of the conversation, shown as a colored dot with a label (for example, *Neutral*). |
| **Status**                 | The conversation's status, such as **Open** or **Closed**. |
| **Last message**           | The date and time of the most recent message. The table is sorted by this column, newest first, by default. |

Rows that need human oversight are tinted in the warning color, so they stand out without a column of their own.

The title, topics and sentiment shown here are produced by the automatic analyses you configure in [Conversations settings](./settings/conversations.mdx). All labels can be re‑run with **Refresh labels**, which is useful after you change your topic list or models in settings.

## Filter the table

Select **Filters** next to the search box to open the filters panel beside the table. It offers:

* **User**, **Touchpoint** and **Topics**: tick one or more values. The **User** list has its own search box, so you can find a person among everyone who has ever chatted.
* **Sentiment** and **Status**: pick one value.
* **Show only**: limit the table to **Favorites** or to conversations that **Needs review**.

Choose **Apply** to filter the table, or **Clear** to empty the panel. The **Filters** button shows how many filters are active, and the applied filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

## Open a conversation

Click any row (or choose **Open** from the row's **Actions** menu) to open the conversation. The table collapses into a narrow list on the left and the thread fills the rest of the page, with the user's messages on the right, the agent's replies labeled with who answered, and operator replies in their own style with a sent or failed state.

The conversation header gives you several tools:

* **Search** within the conversation.
* **Star** to add the conversation to your favorites.
* **Details** opens the details drawer on the right (see below).
* **More conversation actions** holds **Advanced mode**, **Copy conversation ID** and **Delete conversation**. **Advanced mode** exposes the underlying message objects: tool calls, JSON payloads, `tool_call_id`s and any errors the agent encountered. Use it to understand exactly what happened behind a reply.

### The details drawer

The **Details** drawer shows the facts about the conversation in one place: **User** (with a link to all of that user's conversations), **Touchpoint**, **Status**, **Sentiment**, **Started**, **Last message**, number of **Messages**, the conversation **ID**, its **Topics** (with a **Refresh labels** button) and a **Summary**. Summaries aren't stored, so the drawer shows the ones generated during your session and offers **Generate summary** for a new one.

When a conversation is flagged for human oversight, the review decision also sits at the top of this drawer.

### The take-over bar

While the agent is in charge of a conversation, a bar replaces the message box at the bottom. It states who holds the conversation (for example, *"The AI handles this conversation"* or *"The AI is answering the user"*) and offers:

* **Take over:** hand the conversation from the agent to you, so you can reply directly when a user needs personal attention.
* **Stop generating:** end an answer the agent is generating right now, without taking the conversation over.
* **Generate summary:** open an AI summary of the conversation for a quick recap of a long thread.

For the full procedure, see [Take over an AI-managed conversation](/operational-guides/chat-experience#take-over-an-ai-managed-conversation).

## Conversations flagged for human oversight

When your oversight rules decide a conversation warrants human attention, it's marked **Needs review** in the table and a review panel appears at the top of the **Details** drawer. If the drawer is closed, a **Needs review** button in the conversation header opens it. The panel is where the reviewer records what they concluded — **Leave as it is** or **I stepped in** — with an optional note.

Recording a decision resolves the flag, so the conversation leaves the review queue. The panel stays, collapsed to a single line naming the conclusion, so the review is still visible afterwards rather than vanishing along with the flag.

The rules that raise the flag are configured in [Conversations settings](./settings/conversations.mdx); the decision belongs on the conversation itself. For the full workflow, see [Record oversight decisions](/operational-guides/human-oversight).

## Best practices

* **Triage with filters:** combine the **Sentiment** and **Status** filters, or **Show only** conversations that need review, to surface conversations that may need attention.
* **Take over when it counts:** when an agent is struggling or a user asks for a person, use **Take over** rather than letting the thread stall. If the agent is only stuck on a runaway answer, **Stop generating** is enough.
* **Keep labels current:** after editing your topic list or switching models, select the affected conversations and **Refresh labels** so reporting stays accurate.
* **Debug with Advanced mode:** when a reply looks wrong, turn on **Advanced mode** to inspect the tool calls and errors behind it before changing a prompt or model.
