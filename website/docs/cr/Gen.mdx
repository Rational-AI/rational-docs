# Gen

**Gen** is the area of the Control Room where you create content with AI: images, Word documents, PowerPoint decks, web pages, and social posts. Each piece of work is a session that keeps its earlier attempts, so you can go back to any of them. To ask questions about your data, use [Chat](./Chats.mdx) instead.

## Who can use it

The **Gen** tab appears in the top navigation when your [license](/operational-guides/license) includes the Gen module. Roles have no permission row for Gen. What you can open depends on whether you are an administrator:

| Tab | Who can use it |
| :-- | :------------- |
| **Photo & Video** | Every signed-in user. Users who aren't administrators see only the image sessions they started. |
| **Text**, **Slides**, **Canvas**, and **Social** | Administrators only. For other users these tabs are grayed out. |

## Find your way around

Select **Gen** in the top navigation. The page has five tabs, each with its own gallery and a **Generate** tile to start something new.

| Tab | What you create | Guide |
| :-- | :-------------- | :---- |
| **Photo & Video** | Images that start from a template or from the generation defaults. Despite its name, this tab generates images only. | [Generate images](/operational-guides/gen-images) |
| **Text** | Word documents, written by an assistant in a chat beside the file. | [Generate documents](/operational-guides/gen-documents) |
| **Slides** | PowerPoint presentations, written the same way. | [Generate documents](/operational-guides/gen-documents) |
| **Canvas** | An HTML page or an SVG image. | [Generate documents](/operational-guides/gen-documents) |
| **Social** | Posts for Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and X, built step by step from a template. Posts go out through a connector, except X posts, which you download. | [Create and publish social posts](/operational-guides/social-studio) |

The **Canvas** tab in Gen is a separate feature from the **Canvas** option in Chat. In Gen, Canvas is a document session that produces HTML pages and SVG images; in Chat, it is the panel where documents the agent creates open beside the conversation.

## The image gallery

In **Photo & Video**, the gallery groups your images by date. Its header holds:

* **Filters**, to show **Favorites only**, one **Folder**, or one **Period**.
* A search box.
* **Actions**, which appears when you select one or more cards: **Favorite**, **Download**, **Add to folder**, and **Delete**.
* **Guide**, which replays the guided tour. Gen offers the tour the first time you open it.

Every image Gen generates carries an embedded marker that says a model made it. See [How generated images declare they were made by AI](/operational-guides/generated-image-provenance).

## Where Gen is configured

Administrators set up Gen under **Settings** → **Gen**, in the **Capabilities** section: the models it uses, the image defaults, style presets, templates, the document assistants, and the social templates. Users who aren't administrators can open the **Photo & video** tab there, but the other tabs are grayed out for them. See [Configure Gen for your team](/operational-guides/configure-gen).
